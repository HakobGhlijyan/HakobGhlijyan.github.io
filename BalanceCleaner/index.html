Overview

Clear Apple ID Balance

To change your Apple ID region, your account balance must be exactly zero.

STEP 1 Check Your Remaining Balance

Open App Store account settings and find your exact remaining store credit.

STEP 2 Calculate and Purchase

Buy an item or tip in the app. If price exceeds balance, Apple charges the rest from your credit card.

STEP 3 Change Region Checklist

Step-by-step checklist to verify zero balance, subscriptions canceled, and prepare for region change.

STEP 4 Settings & Contact Support

Access settings, view transaction history, or contact developer and Apple Support if assistance is needed.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is BalanceCleaner?
BalanceCleaner lets you easily select or enter the exact custom amount to zero out your Apple ID account balance — no account registration required.
How does the balance split work?
See the precise breakdown of your remaining balance and calculated parts before confirming any operation. Everything is shown clearly before you proceed.
Is my data safe?
Yes. All processing happens locally on your device via Apple's StoreKit framework. We do not collect, store or upload any personal data.
Do I need an account?
No account is required. Download and start immediately.
What is Pro?
Pro unlocks full transaction history, PDF export, and advanced settings. The core cleaner is free for everyone.
What is the Region Checklist?
A step-by-step guide to prepare your account for a region change. It checks if your Apple ID balance is zero, subscriptions are canceled, and Family Sharing is left.
What Pro customization is available?
Pro users can change the App background to Starfield and customize the Amount Ring Apple Glow Intent Design.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 · Zero tracking policy

🍎
Apple StoreKit Secure
All balance clearing operations run through official Apple In-App Purchase APIs.
🛡️
Zero Payment Access
We never see, collect, or store your Apple ID password, credit cards, or billing info.
🔒
No Data Sales
Your personal details are never sold, rented, or shared with third-party advertisers.
1. Scope & Core Purpose
This Privacy Policy applies to the BalanceCleaner iOS application and this website. The app is designed exclusively to help users zero out leftover Apple ID balances (such as before changing App Store region) through verified in-app purchases.
2. Apple ID & StoreKit 2 Processing
BalanceCleaner operates strictly within Apple’s native StoreKit 2 framework. All financial transactions are processed directly and securely by Apple Inc. The developer never receives access to your Apple ID password, payment credentials, credit card details, or full billing history.
3. Information We Collect (Zero Personal Data)
The BalanceCleaner app does not collect personal information. It contains no advertising, no third-party tracking libraries, and no analytics SDKs. We do not receive your Apple ID, payment methods, or cleanup history.
4. On-Device Storage & iCloud Sync
All transaction logs and app settings are stored locally in on-device sandbox storage. If you choose to enable iCloud sync, data synchronizes exclusively through Apple CloudKit within your private Apple ID container. The developer has zero access to this private database.
5. Website & Support Communications
This website uses browser localStorage solely to remember your language choice. When you send a message through the support form, your name, email, and message are forwarded directly to our email via FormSubmit solely to respond to your request.
6. GDPR, CCPA & Permanent Deletion Rights
Under GDPR and CCPA regulations, you have the full right to inspect, export, or permanently delete any support communications sent to us. Simply contact us at balancecleanerapp@gmail.com to request immediate deletion.
7. Contact & Developer Information
BalanceCleaner is developed independently by Hakob Ghlijyan, an iOS engineer based in Yerevan, Armenia. For any privacy or technical questions, please email balancecleanerapp@gmail.com.
