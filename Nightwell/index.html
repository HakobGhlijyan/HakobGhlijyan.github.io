About Nightwell

A calm sleep tracker built around a rhythm you choose.

Nightwell helps you sleep on your own schedule without noise, subscriptions traps, or intrusive accounts. Set your bedtime and wake time, press one button when you go to sleep and see the night take shape. In the morning rate how you slept and track what affected it: coffee, stress, exercise, a late meal or room temperature.

Built by Hakob Ghlijyan, an independent iOS developer based in Yerevan, Armenia. Nightwell is crafted natively with Swift and SwiftUI, honoring Apple design principles and respecting user privacy at every touchpoint.

Frequently Asked Questions

What devices and software do I need?
You need an iPhone running iOS 26.1 or later. An Apple Watch with watchOS 26.1 or later is completely optional.
The alarm did not ring. What should I check?
Check that alarm permissions are granted (Settings → Night Well), that the night session was started, and that Wake Alarm is turned on in app Settings. The alarm uses Apple AlarmKit and sounds even on silent mode.
What is the early wake-up window?
It is an optional 15 or 30-minute window before your scheduled alarm. The app may wake you at the end of a typical sleep cycle. It is an estimate and will never sound later than your wake time.
How does iCloud synchronization work?
Sessions, settings, and ratings sync through your personal private iCloud account using CloudKit. The developer has zero access to your data.
How is Apple Health data used?
Writing finished nights to Apple Health is optional. Reading sleep stages from Apple Watch is off by default and only displayed on screen: it is never saved, synced, or sent anywhere.
How can I export or delete my data?
You can export your nights as CSV or JSON in Settings → Export Data. To delete all data: delete the app, then remove iCloud storage in iOS Settings.
Is Nightwell a medical device?
No. Nightwell is an informational sleep tracker and alarm. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat any medical conditions.

Nightwell is a sleep tracker and not a medical device. Requirements: iOS 26.1 / watchOS 26.1 or later.
