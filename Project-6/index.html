Frequently Asked Questions

What is Project 6?
Project 6 is a calm, fast photo editing application for iPhone and iPad with studio-grade Core Image filters, non-destructive versions, crop ratios, and custom watermarks.
Are my photos ever uploaded to external servers?
Never. All photo processing happens entirely on your device using Apple Core Image. Syncing occurs only through your private iCloud database managed by Apple.
Do I need an account?
No account is required. Download and start editing immediately without registration.
How does non-destructive versioning work?
Your original photograph is never modified or overwritten. Every time you save an edit, Project 6 saves a new version in your gallery while preserving the pristine original.
What watermark styles are available?
Project 6 includes eight watermark styles derived from the photo EXIF data, timestamp, camera model, or custom text with full color selection.
Which devices and iOS versions are supported?
Project 6 supports all iPhone and iPad models running iOS 18.0, iPadOS 18.0, or newer, optimized for Liquid Glass and modern Super Retina displays.
How do I request a feature or report a bug?
You can submit feedback directly via our web support form or email hakobghlijyan@gmail.com. We review every user message.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 · Native iOS 18+ Architecture

Zero Personal Data Collection

Project 6 was designed from the ground up to respect your digital privacy. We do not require accounts, we do not track your activity, and we never transmit your photos to third-party servers.

1. Summary
Project 6 has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Your photos, edits and their metadata stay on your device. If you turn on iCloud, they sync only through your own private iCloud, which the developer cannot access.
2. Who We Are
Project 6 is developed by Hakob Ghlijyan, an independent developer. Contact: hakobghlijyan@gmail.com.
3. What the App Stores
Photos you import, the edited versions you save, their metadata (date, camera, location if the photo has it), albums, favourites and your settings. All of it is stored in the app’s own storage on your device.
4. iCloud
Sync is optional. When enabled, the app uses Apple’s private CloudKit database tied to your Apple ID. Only you can access it; the developer has no access and no copy. You can switch sync off in Settings.
5. Photos
You pick photos with the system photo picker. The app asks for access to Photos once, only to read the location of the photos you picked, and, if you tap Save to Photos, to add edited images. It never scans or uploads your library.
6. Location
Location is read only from the metadata that is already inside a photo. To show a place name, the app may send those coordinates to Apple’s geocoding service. Nothing is sent to the developer.
7. What We Do NOT Do
No tracking, no advertising, no third-party analytics or SDKs, no selling or sharing of data, no account, no profiling.
8. Diagnostic Logs
The app writes no logs that leave your device. If you share crash reports with Apple through iOS settings, Apple may provide anonymous reports to developers.
9. Data Export and Deletion
Sharing and exporting keep your photo’s metadata intact — the choice of where to send it is yours. Deleted photos stay 3 days in Recently Deleted. With iCloud on, deleting a photo also deletes it on your other devices. Deleting the app removes its local data.
10. Children
The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.
11. Security
Data stays in the app’s protected storage on your device and, optionally, in your private iCloud, both protected by iOS.
12. This Website
This site is static. It sets no tracking cookies and uses no analytics. If you write through the contact form, we use your name, email and message only to reply.
13. Contact Information
For questions regarding this privacy policy, please contact Hakob Ghlijyan directly at hakobghlijyan@gmail.com.
