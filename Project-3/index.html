Getting started (4 steps)

  1. Tap + in the Editor and pick a photo.
  2. Choose a filter, crop, mirror or add a watermark.
  3. Save — every save becomes a new version in Photos.
  4. Find it in Photos or Collections, then share or save to Photos.

Frequently Asked Questions

What devices and software do I need?
An iPhone or iPad with iOS 18 or later.
Is my original photo changed?
No. Every edit is saved as a separate version; the original stays untouched.
How does iCloud sync work?
Turn it on in Settings → iCloud. Sign in with the same Apple ID on all devices. Sync uses your private CloudKit database.
Some photos do not sync. What should I do?
They are safe on your device. Ensure you have free iCloud storage and keep the app open on Wi‑Fi.
I deleted a photo by mistake.
Open Collections → Recently Deleted. Photos stay there for 3 days and can be restored.
Is location kept when I share or export?
Yes. Sharing and exporting keep all EXIF metadata, including location, unchanged.
Does Filter Studio send my photos anywhere?
No. Photos stay on your device and, if you enable it, in your private iCloud. Zero external servers.
Privacy

Privacy Policy

The app does not collect your personal data. Zero accounts, zero ads, and zero analytics.

1. Summary: Filter Studio has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Your photos, edits and their metadata stay on your device.

2. Who we are: Filter Studio is developed by Hakob Ghlijyan, an independent developer. Contact: hakobghlijyan@gmail.com.

3. What the app stores: Photos you import, edited versions, metadata (date, camera, location), albums, and your settings inside local on-device sandbox storage.

4. iCloud: Sync is optional. The app uses Apple's private CloudKit database tied to your Apple ID. The developer has zero access.

5. Photos: You select images through the system photo picker. The app never scans or uploads your photo library.

6. Location: Location is read strictly from existing EXIF metadata. To display place names, coordinates may query Apple's native geocoder.

7. What we do not do: No tracking, no advertising, no third-party analytics SDKs, and no selling or profiling of user data.

8. Diagnostic logs: The app writes zero logs that leave your device.

9. Data export and deletion: Deleted photos remain 3 days in Recently Deleted before permanent removal. Deleting the app deletes all local data.

10. Children: The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

11. Security: Data is protected by Apple hardware encryption and private iCloud architecture.

12. This website: Hosted on GitHub Pages with Google Fonts. Form submissions are processed securely via Web3Forms solely to reply to your inquiry.

13. Contact: For any privacy questions, reach out directly to hakobghlijyan@gmail.com.
